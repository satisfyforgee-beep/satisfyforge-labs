What the owned system is built to do

01

One upload, platform-specific output

Store one source video and keep each network's caption, title, disclosure and privacy settings separately.

02

Automatic publishing

Scheduled jobs send due content through official platform APIs after the account owner authorizes each network.

03

Duplicate protection

Published state is recorded per network so ambiguous failures are checked instead of blindly reposting the same asset.

04

AI-content flags

Carry supported disclosure fields for AI-generated or significantly edited media rather than hiding how the content was made.

05

Owned queue and logs

Your schedule and post state live in the SatisfyForge system instead of disappearing when a third-party scheduler plan changes.

06

No required Buffer/Metricool subscription

Third-party schedulers can remain optional fallbacks, but the product is designed so they are not the permanent publishing layer.
