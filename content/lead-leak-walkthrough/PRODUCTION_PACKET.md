## Final 27-second script

### 0:00–0:03 — Hook

**Voiceover:** “Your contact form could be losing customers before they ever contact you.”

**On-screen text:** YOUR CONTACT FORM MAY BE LOSING CUSTOMERS

### 0:03–0:10 — Show the leak

**Voiceover:** “Too many required fields, a weak submit button, and no clue what happens next all add friction.”

**Visual:** Show the fictional nine-field form. Punch in on required fields, the gray **SUBMIT** button, and “We will contact you soon.”

**On-screen counters:**

- 9 required fields
- vague next step
- no response time

### 0:10–0:20 — Show the fix

**Voiceover:** “Start with the essentials: who they are, how to reach them, and what service they need. Then tell them when you’ll respond.”

**Visual:** Activate **SHOW THE FIX**. Show the reduced form and green **REQUEST MY ESTIMATE** button.

**On-screen counters:**

- fewer fields
- clear action
- response expectation

### 0:20–0:27 — CTA

**Voiceover:** “More traffic won’t fix a broken contact process. Find the leaks first.”

**On-screen text:** FREE REVENUE LEAK AUDIT · LINK IN BIO

**End card:** SatisfyForge · Watch. Play. Explore. Build.
