# Skill Vault pilot venue pack

## What the venue gets
- Compact skill-based reaction kiosk designed for short repeat play.
- Merchandise-prize progression driven by visible player score rather than hidden chance.
- SatisfyForge-managed software, maintenance plan and operating ledger.
- A future approved placement can use a negotiated revenue share; the prototype planning model uses 70% SatisfyForge / 30% venue before final real-cost validation.

## What the venue provides
- Approved placement area, standard power, basic supervision and access for maintenance.
- Compliance with the written prize-handling and operating procedure.
- No independent changes to software, payout formula, prize catalog or payment hardware.

## Pilot success criteria
- Stable uptime and verified scoring.
- No unresolved tamper or reconciliation issues.
- Positive unit economics after actual venue share, merchandise, processing, taxes, maintenance, insurance and regulatory costs.
- Player interest measured through repeat plays and completed sessions without deceptive retention tactics.

## Not yet for sale/live placement
This pack is planning material. The current build processes no real payment and awards no real prize. Do not promise a live Ohio placement date until the exact machine/operator/location approval requirements are cleared.
