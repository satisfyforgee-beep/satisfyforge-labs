SATISFYFORGE CONTRACTOR LEAD RESCUE KIT
Version 1.0

Purpose
-------
A plug-and-play follow-up system for contractors and local service businesses that lose quote requests, missed calls, old estimates and past customers because nobody follows up consistently.

What is included
----------------
1. lead-rescue-playbook.html — the complete playbook and copy library.
2. follow-up-calendar.csv — a simple contact schedule you can import into a spreadsheet/CRM.
3. quick-start.txt — the 20-minute implementation checklist.

How to use it
-------------
- Replace bracketed placeholders like [FIRST NAME], [SERVICE] and [BUSINESS NAME].
- Start with the Day 0 / Day 2 / Day 5 / Day 10 quote-recovery sequence.
- Use the missed-call, no-show, review and reactivation sections only when relevant.
- Keep messages conversational and truthful. Do not invent urgency, discounts or availability.
- Honor opt-outs and applicable marketing/communications laws and platform rules.

This product is a business communication template kit, not legal advice and not a guarantee of sales.
