SATISFYFORGE — 20-MINUTE LEAD RESCUE QUICK START

1) Pick ONE service that matters most financially.
2) Export or list the last 20-50 qualified inquiries that did not book.
3) Remove anyone who opted out, is clearly not a fit, or should not be contacted.
4) Personalize the Day 0 message in the playbook.
5) Send it manually to a small batch first.
6) Tag replies: BOOKED / INTERESTED / LATER / NO / WRONG FIT.
7) Two days later, send the Day 2 message only to people who did not reply.
8) On Day 5, offer a real next step: available appointment, estimate slot, inspection, or answer to a question.
9) On Day 10, send the respectful close-the-loop message.
10) Measure: replies, appointments, booked jobs, booked revenue.

DO NOT measure success by opens alone. The score that matters is booked gross profit.
