## Short description
Collect glowing Brainrot, chain combos, upgrade your earnings, rebirth for permanent boosts, and survive surprise Snackquake events in a fast neon arena.

## Full description
Brainrot Rush is a fast arcade collection game built around quick runs and simple progression. Race around a neon arena collecting Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary Brainrot. Keep your combo alive for better rewards, spend earnings on upgrades, and reach the rebirth requirement to reset for a permanent multiplier.

Every so often a Snackquake shakes up the normal loop with faster spawns and boosted rewards. The core game is designed to remain fully playable without purchases.

Current release candidate features:
- Server-authoritative pickups and progression
- Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary Brainrot
- Combo rewards
- Upgrade and rebirth progression
- Timed Snackquake events
- Saved progression
- Multiplayer support
- Mobile-friendly HUD layouts

## Suggested genre / positioning
Fast arcade / collection / progression.
