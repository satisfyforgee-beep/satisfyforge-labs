# Rift Raiders v0.11 — Social Citizens

The living downtown population is now more recognizable and interactive.

## Added
- Verified classic Roblox hair accessories on all 8 R15 downtown citizens.
- Named TALK proximity prompts.
- Contextual dialogue for employment, shopping, creator gear, needs, properties, businesses, police and world travel.
- Server-side downtown conversation counter.

## Safety / progression
Citizen conversations are informational only. They do not provide free currency, ownership, job completion or progression bypasses. Purchases, work and rewards remain server-authoritative.

## Current limits
Dialogue is not yet a relationship system and citizens do not remember individual players. Pedestrian movement is still repeated MoveTo routing rather than full crowd pathfinding. No Robux products or public publishing are activated.
