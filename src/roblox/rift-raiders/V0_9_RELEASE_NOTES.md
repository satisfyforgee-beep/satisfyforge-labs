# Rift Raiders v0.9 — Downtown Comes Alive

This release turns the official Modern City environment added in v0.8 into a functional life-simulation district.

## Added
- 6 downtown storefronts tied to real in-game shopping categories.
- 1 downtown employment center serving all 20 careers.
- 20 career worksites and 38 task anchors distributed around the imported city block.
- Creator recording, business revenue collection, court payments, public food/rest services and 2 additional police patrols in downtown.
- 48 total life-simulation purchases, including expanded tech, clothing, home, property and business options.
- 9 business ownership types with recurring simulated revenue.
- LIFE menu split into Careers and Shopping, with shopping category filters.

## Validation
All career selection, work, shopping and business actions remain server-authoritative and proximity-checked. No paid Robux products or public publishing were activated.
