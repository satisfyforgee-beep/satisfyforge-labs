## What this test really demonstrates
- Proximity-only NPC dialogue is available while away-from-NPC requests fail, without currency rewards.
- The parcel is spawned at legitimate pickup, welded to the right hand, massless, non-colliding and labeled with the server's assigned SKU. A second player's character does not receive it.
- Deleting the server-owned parcel prevents delivery and tip, and the player can recollect the same parcel at dispatch without changing the assignment or earning free coins.
- Successful delivery removes the carried package; replaying delivery does not pay twice.
- `Life.CharacterRespawned` resets an unfinished courier pickup after respawn; this code path was added but not explicitly exercised by the two-player runtime script, and remains a targeted future regression.
