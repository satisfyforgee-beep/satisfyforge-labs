# Rift Raiders — $0 Launch and Monetization Plan

## The business reality
A Roblox game earns money only if people choose to play and spend, or if it qualifies for platform reward programs. A functioning game and AI-written code do not guarantee visits, retention, Robux, or cash income. No Robux purchases are currently implemented, and no spending is authorized.

## Priorities before monetization
1. Fix core gameplay and prove the first minute is fun on a real Samsung Android device.
2. Test with 5–10 willing players; observe onboarding confusion, errors, return intent and repeat play.
3. Add reliable live saving, ownership validation, safe rejoin behavior and cross-server consistency.
4. Add memorable boss animation, attack telegraphs, rewarding enemy variety and distinct island activities.
5. Build original screenshots, icon and thumbnail from the actual game, not fake screenshots.
6. Publish only after the owner verifies account, privacy, experience guidelines and final content.

## Free organic acquisition
- Short gameplay clips (15–30 seconds) showing actual boss fights, close calls and player-created moments.
- Behind-the-scenes build diary showing genuine before/after improvements.
- Poll players on one feature per video, then implement and demonstrate the winner.
- Invite relevant Roblox creators or communities to a voluntary playtest. No spam, fake accounts or fake metrics.
- Measure qualified visits and engagement rather than follower counts alone.

## Proposed optional cosmetic monetization (requires approval, not built)
- Cosmetic Riftling skins and glow trails: one-time game passes after verified ownership.
- Celebration emotes and nameplate effects: one-time game passes, never necessary to progress.
- Seasonal non-random cosmetic bundles: transparent value, no deceptive countdowns.
- Avoid pay-to-win, paid random rewards, misleading price displays and interruptive prompts.
- If any developer products are eventually added, use Roblox MarketplaceService.ProcessReceipt with receipt deduplication and durable entitlement grants before enabling purchases.
- Actual prices, product IDs and final offerings are undecided.

## Metrics to instrument after private publishing
- New-player onboarding: join -> first shard -> first drone -> first rift seal.
- First-session completion and voluntary replay rate.
- D1 and D7 retention, crash and mobile performance.
- Economy balance: earned coins per minute, sinks, upgrade adoption.
- Monetization only after engagement: conversion rate, ARPPU and ARPDAU (Roblox Analytics).
- Do not claim any of these metrics exist until collected from actual players.
