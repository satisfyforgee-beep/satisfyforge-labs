# Rift Raiders v0.28 — Floor Height & House Arrival QA

**Date:** October 18, 2026, Eastern. Private test build, not publicly released.

## Root cause and measured results

A temporary **real Studio world-geometry audit** of the v0.27 build found that aligning imported whole-model bounding-box minima with the terrain was not sufficient. The two original furnished suburban house templates contain low decorative geometry that distorts the bbox reference. Actual entrance controls remained implausibly high above grass:

| Imported original house | v0.27 entrance marker Y | v0.28 entrance marker Y | Ground-floor surface used for placement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Single-story house | 20.2166 | 6.1001 | 1.8 |
| Two-story house | 27.6762 | 6.1001 | 1.8 |

Measurements are engine-side results from `tests/roblox/rift_raiders_navigation_audit.luau` (temporary initial investigation script, checked using Studio RunScript) and first-floor assertions in the official smoke suite. They are **world Y coordinates in Roblox studs**, not distances from the entrance to the grass or frame-rate measurements.

The fixed floor target is Y=1.8, consistent with ground grass approximately Y=.5 and roads approximately Y=1.4. The door control markers' centers are above that walking surface (not at shoe height). Original interior furniture, frames, windows, roof geometry, and doors are translated together.

## Remaining limits

- Passing geometry assertions and scripted player proximity tests do **not** prove a human can freely navigate from every curb to every furnished room, upper-story bedroom, shop aisle or stairway. No actual guided screen/camera walk-through of those paths was completed in this release.
- Simple concrete walkway surfaces aren't professionally detailed landscaping, and formal accessibility compliance has not been established.
- Real low-end Android/iOS FPS, server memory/crowd stress, live multi-server DataStore leave/rejoin, account-level mesh/texture permissions, moderation and five independent human first-time playtests remain unmet launch gates.
- Studio still intermittently reported third-party asset noncompliance warnings (e.g. asset `rbxassetid://92546828372423` in one audit run, exact asset origin not verified). Roblox UI `RoundedCorner8` style warnings also appeared. Neither is asserted fixed.
- An actively edited historical `v0.21` Studio release artifact and Studio `.lock` files were deliberately preserved and excluded from this work. No public publishing, Robux or live data modification.
