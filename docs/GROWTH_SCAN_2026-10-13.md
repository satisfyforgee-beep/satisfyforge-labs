# SatisfyForge Growth Scan — 2026-10-13

## What changed materially

### YouTube Shorts: originality matters even more

On October 1, 2026, TeamYouTube announced that Shorts recommendations are being updated to further prioritize original content and reduce reach for channels that mainly aggregate or re-upload other creators' videos without meaningful added value.

Operational implication for SatisfyForge:
- Keep every factual Short original in script, narration, edit, visual sequencing and point of view.
- Do not build a growth strategy around ripped clips, compilations or lightly modified reposts.
- Public-domain/reference material may inform research, but the execution should be genuinely transformed into an original SatisfyForge story.

Official source: https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/470890423

### YouTube monetization: treat Shorts as a funnel, not the whole business

Current expanded-YPP entry remains 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in 90 days + either 3,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 3M qualified Shorts views in 90 days for earlier monetization features such as fan funding and Shopping.

Current ad/Premium threshold remains 1,000 subscribers + either 4,000 qualified watch hours or 10M qualified Shorts views.

Starting February 1, 2027, new applicants for ads/Premium will face 8,000 qualified watch hours or 20M qualified Shorts views, still with 1,000 subscribers. Earlier fan-funding/Shopping thresholds remain unchanged.

Operational implication:
- Use Shorts to grow an audience and route qualified viewers toward verified SatisfyForge products/services rather than planning around Shorts ad revenue alone.
- The verified Creator Kit offer (MSLBK, last public check $7.99) is a better immediate monetization destination than waiting for ad thresholds.
- Lead Rescue/local-business services remain another direct first-dollar path.

Official sources:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009

### TikTok Creator Rewards: longer originals create another option

TikTok's Creator Rewards Program rewards high-quality original content over one minute and weighs originality, play duration, search value and audience engagement.

Operational implication:
- Keep the existing 20–35 second factual-story lane because it fits Shorts/Reels/TikTok discovery well.
- After the fixed Dancing Plague / First Computer Bug backlog, test one strong 60–90 second original deeper story as a separate experiment rather than stretching every video.
- Do not sacrifice hook density or completion merely to cross one minute.

Official source: https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program

### Roblox: make the next Studio session more productive

Roblox Studio now has a beta Device Simulator toolbar and Network Simulator. Device Simulator can preview phone/tablet/desktop/console/headset behavior; Network Simulator can add latency, jitter and packet loss. Roblox also documents Studio-only scripted testing services for multi-client/device/input tests.

Operational implication:
- When Studio access is available, use Device Simulator for portrait/landscape/touch checks and Network Simulator for Wi-Fi/mobile/bad-connection checks in the same validation session.
- This does not remove the need for a real Studio session, but it reduces the amount of separate physical hardware needed for coverage.

Official sources:
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/studio/device-simulator
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/studio/network-simulator
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/studio/testing-modes

### Roblox DevEx tax change

Beginning November 1, 2026, Roblox says DevEx payments are classified as royalties under updated terms and creator tax information moves into Creator Hub. Roblox recommends creators review/update tax information before the change; October 31 is the final day under the prior terms.

Operational implication:
- Treat any eventual DevEx tax setup as an owner-only step.
- Never put tax identifiers into ChatGPT, GitHub or public project files.
- This is not an immediate blocker because no SatisfyForge Roblox game is publicly earning yet.

Official source: https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information

2. **Verified digital product:** promote the Creator Kit organically when an appropriate original story/tutorial asset is ready; do not confuse it with the paused Tool Vault.

4. **Length experiment:** test one 60–90 second high-quality original story after the currently scheduled backlog clears.

6. **Roblox:** continue cloud-side source/docs work, then use the new Studio device/network tooling when a Studio-capable machine is available.
