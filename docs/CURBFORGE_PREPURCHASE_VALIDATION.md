# CurbForge Pre-Purchase Validation

Recovered into the Master Command Center on 2026-10-13 from the existing CurbForge demand-test and first-mailbox packages.

## Rule

Do **not** buy a printer, materials, hardware, ads, marketplace listings or paid software just to find out whether people like the idea. Validate credible demand at realistic prices first.

Do **not** take deposits while the production process, printer ownership and fulfillment timing are still unverified. Use concept / interest-list language only.

## Existing product assets

### First custom vehicle mailbox
The existing `CurbForge_First_Mailbox_v02.zip` contains printable prototype modules:

The visual/product standard remains: look realistically straight off a good multicolor 3D printer, with modular geometry, visible printable detail and replaceable parts. Do not market glossy injection-molded/painted imagery as if it is the raw printed product.

## Demand tests

### 1. Custom vehicle mailbox
Concept price:
- starting around **$449**
- exact-vehicle replica concept around **$599**

Meaningful validation signal:
- at least 5 serious inquiries about a buyer's own vehicle
- at least 2 credible people comfortable with the displayed price
- voluntary vehicle-photo submissions are a stronger signal than likes alone

### 2. House-replica mailbox
Concept price:
- starting around **$799**
- highly detailed custom concept around **$949**

Meaningful validation signal:
- at least 3 serious homeowner inquiries
- at least 1 credible buyer comfortable with the displayed price
- customers voluntarily offer house photos for a replica

### 3. Insulated personalized cat house
Concept price:
- starting around **$299**
- personalized premium concept around **$349**

Meaningful validation signal:
- at least 5 serious inquiries
- questions about dimensions, weather resistance or insulation rather than only "cute"
- at least 2 credible buyers comfortable around $299+

## One-week test structure

This schedule is a **content-test template**, not a standing automation and not permission to post to unrelated SatisfyForge channels.

- Day 1: vehicle-mailbox hero concept
- Day 2: vehicle alternate angle + how access works
- Day 3: house-replica hero
- Day 4: cat-house hero
- Day 5: three-product comparison — ask which should be built first
- Weekend: answer legitimate questions and collect voluntary photos / product preferences

Track:
- views
- saves
- shares
- comments
- serious inquiries
- price objections
- voluntary photo submissions
- explicit "I would buy this at that price" responses
- requested models/colors
- most-requested product

## Equipment purchase gate

A printer purchase becomes materially easier to justify only after at least **3 credible premium buyers** emerge across the test and the likely first products can cover their own direct material/hardware cost with a healthy margin.

If visuals perform but serious interest disappears when price is shown, fix the product, positioning or price **before** spending money on equipment.

## Product-safety / fulfillment boundaries

- Mailbox concepts should preserve a compliant functional mailbox core rather than assuming a fully printed shell alone is postal-compliant.
- Outdoor products need real ASA/weather/UV/drainage testing before weatherproof claims.
- Cat-house heating should use a protected cord path for a customer-supplied pet-safe product; do not build or improvise mains-powered heating hardware.
- Do not promise shipment dates before printer throughput and failure rate are measured.
