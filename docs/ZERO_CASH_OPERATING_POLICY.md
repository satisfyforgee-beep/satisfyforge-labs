# SatisfyForge Zero-Cash Operating Policy

SatisfyForge should default to models where customers, sponsors, marketplaces, or revenue partners fund the work — not us.

## Hard rules

1. **No upfront paid software** when a free/self-hosted option can do the job.
2. **No paid trials** that can auto-renew.
3. **No ad spend from our pocket.** Paid promotion is allowed only if a sponsor/client prepays it or the spend is automatically deducted from money they already funded.
4. **No inventory purchases before demand.** Prefer digital delivery, print-on-demand, dropship/fulfillment partners, preorder, made-to-order, or deposits.
5. **No creator-tool credits from our pocket.** Use free generation quotas, owned code, open-source tooling, or customer-funded project fees.
6. **No speculative shipping/material costs.** Physical jobs should collect a deposit that covers materials and unavoidable third-party expenses before those costs are incurred.
7. **No bought followers, likes, views, reviews, or engagement.** Grow through content, distribution, affiliates, partnerships, SEO, referrals, and customer value.
8. **No hidden fees to customers.** Any customer-funded expense must be disclosed in the offer/contract/checkout when required.

## Preferred money flows

- Digital product sale -> platform/payment fee is deducted from the sale -> SatisfyForge keeps the remainder.
- Service job -> deposit/payment arrives first -> materials or subcontractors are paid from that collected money.
- Print-on-demand -> customer pays -> fulfillment provider takes production/shipping cost -> profit is paid out.
- Affiliate/revenue share -> merchant collects money -> SatisfyForge receives commission.
- Sponsorship -> sponsor prepays or contractually funds production/distribution -> deliverables are produced.
- Marketplace/gig work -> client funds escrow/milestone -> work begins -> platform releases payment.
- Software/tool access -> free tier/self-host until revenue supports an upgrade; upgrades are not automatic.

## Decision rule

Before any external spend, ask:

**Has money for this exact expense already been received from a customer/sponsor/partner, or can the expense be deducted automatically from revenue already generated?**

If no, do not spend. Find a free/self-hosted workaround, change the offer, collect a deposit/preorder, or defer the expense.

## Exceptions

There is no standing exception for convenience, speed, advertising, subscriptions, creator credits, inventory, or equipment. A future exception requires explicit owner approval for that exact charge before it happens.
