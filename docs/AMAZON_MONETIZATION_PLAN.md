## Track 2 — Amazon Associates

### Why application is deferred

Amazon's current Associates application review guidance says:
- at least **3 qualifying sales within the first 180 days** are required before application review;
- personal orders do not qualify;
- submitted sites/pages must contain robust original content;
- Amazon gives a rule-of-thumb of **at least 10 posts**;
- website content should be recent, generally within the last 60 days;
- accepted social networks currently include Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube, TikTok and Twitch;
- Amazon says social-network applicants generally need a substantive organic following, in most cases **at least 500**.

Under those rules, applying too early creates avoidable failure risk. SatisfyForge should first build real public original content and traffic.

### Associates readiness gate

Do not apply merely because an account can be created. Apply when all are true:
1. At least 10 strong original public posts/videos exist on the exact properties we will submit.
2. The public properties are active/recent and not behind a paywall.
3. Traffic/following is credible enough to plausibly produce three non-personal qualifying Amazon orders inside 180 days.
4. There is a natural Amazon-product category that fits the existing audience instead of random affiliate spam.
5. Disclosure language and link-placement process are ready.

### Best-fit future SatisfyForge affiliate angles

Use only when the content itself creates a legitimate recommendation context, for example:
- creator equipment actually relevant to the Creator Kit audience
- books/source material related to history/science storytelling
- business/cleaning equipment only on a dedicated relevant business property, not mixed randomly into entertainment posts
- 3D-printing tools/materials only in the separate CurbForge maker context

Do not create thin 'best product' pages purely to generate affiliate clicks.

### Paid ads warning

Amazon's April 2026 operating-agreement update expanded disqualified purchases to include purchases referred through paid/boosted ads linking to Amazon, subject to limited exceptions. The current SatisfyForge $0 rule already avoids paid boosts, so do not build an Associates plan around paid ads.
