## Store quality checklist
- Confirm exact live product names and prices before editing.
- Check that each digital product has its intended ZIP attached and opens correctly.
- Keep the best complete listing for each distinct product; do not delete duplicates without inspecting downloads and purchase links.
- Confirm store visibility, payment connection, and delivery through a legitimate test.
- Avoid promises of guaranteed earnings or results.
