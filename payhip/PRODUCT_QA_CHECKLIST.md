# Payhip product QA

For each product:
- Record product URL, listing visibility, price, and exact file name.
- Download and inspect ZIP for expected files, file corruption, and readme.
- Confirm description accurately matches included content.
- Verify cover art legibility on mobile.
- Confirm checkout and payment status in Payhip dashboard.
- Check duplicate products for distinct files or existing external links before removing any.
- Do not alter existing products without an authenticated browser session and explicit confirmation for deletion.
- Spend $0; avoid test purchases that incur fees without approval.

## Known observations (screenshots, 2026-10-12)
- Holiday Paycheck Command Center editor showed a ZIP (~42 KB), $7.99 price, images, and Visible selection.
- Store setup checklist showed product and checkout completed, store design not completed.
- Product delivery and live payment were not independently tested.
