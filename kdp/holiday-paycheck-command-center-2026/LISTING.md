## Short description
Plan Christmas from the paycheck up. Holiday Paycheck Command Center 2026 is a practical seasonal budgeting planner built for people who want to organize gifts, food, travel, events, online orders, returns and January bills without losing track of the money they actually have available.

## Long description
Holiday spending gets difficult when gifts, food, travel, events, shipping and ordinary bills all compete for the same paycheck. Holiday Paycheck Command Center 2026 gives the reader one place to plan the season from real take-home money instead of guesses.

Inside the planner:
- 12 paycheck-by-paycheck planning pages
- a master holiday budget
- gift and purchase tracking
- online-order and return tracking
- food, hosting, travel, decor and event budgets
- debt guardrails and January-protection pages
- a post-holiday reset
- a next-year holiday sinking-fund section

The system is intentionally simple: cover required bills first, set a holiday ceiling, assign each paycheck before spending, log purchases as they happen and protect January before the final holiday purchase.

No investment advice, debt-settlement promise or savings guarantee is made. This is an organizational planner.
