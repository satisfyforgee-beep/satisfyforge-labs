# SatisfyForge Outreach Reply Fulfillment Kit

Purpose: fulfill the free samples promised by the active Hunter sequences quickly and usefully after a prospect replies. This is a fulfillment asset, not a mass-send script.

## Operating rules

1. Personalize to the prospect's actual company, website, services and stated reply.
2. Give useful material first. Do not turn the sample into another vague pitch.
3. Never invent a missed-lead count, conversion rate, revenue number, testimonial or result.
4. If a prospect replies with a specific problem, solve that problem instead of forcing the generic sample below.
5. End with one low-pressure next step: offer to map or implement the system if they want help.
6. Stop automated outreach when a real reply arrives and handle the conversation manually/personalized.

## Simple tracking map

Track only what helps the next action:
- New inquiry date/time
- Name + phone/email
- Service requested
- First response sent? timestamp
- Replied?
- Estimate/appointment set?
- Follow-up stage: Day 2 / Day 5 / Day 10 / reactivation
- Final status: booked / not now / lost / no response
- Reason lost when known

## Personalization pass before sending

Replace generic terms with the company's real high-value service where obvious. Roofing examples: roof inspection, storm damage, leak, replacement. HVAC examples: no-cool/no-heat, repair, replacement, maintenance. Restoration examples: water loss, fire/smoke, mold, emergency mitigation. Never imply 24/7 service unless the company actually offers it.

# B. Lead Follow-Up Automation Sample

Use for replies to sequence 904608.

## Four-message follow-up map

### Message 1 — immediately after inquiry

Hi {{first_name_or_there}} — thanks for contacting {{company}} about {{need}}. I want to make sure this doesn't sit in an inbox. What's the best number/time to reach you, and is there anything important we should know before we call?

### Message 2 — next business day

Hi {{first_name_or_there}} — following up on your request with {{company}}. Do you still want help with {{need}}? If yes, reply with the best time to connect and we'll take it from there.

### Message 3 — a few days later

Hi {{first_name_or_there}} — one quick check before I close this out. If timing, budget or scheduling is the reason you haven't moved forward, tell me which one and I'll point you to the most useful next step.

### Message 4 — final close-the-loop

Hi {{first_name_or_there}} — last follow-up from {{company}} on this request. Reply **OPEN** if you want us to keep it active; otherwise we'll close it so you don't keep getting messages.

## Lightweight automation map

A useful first version does not need expensive software:
1. New inquiry enters one tracker.
2. Immediate response is triggered or sent from a saved template.
3. Lead gets a next-follow-up date.
4. Day 1 / later follow-ups happen only while status remains open.
5. Any real reply, booking, opt-out or closed status stops the sequence.
6. A weekly view shows open leads, overdue follow-ups, booked leads and lost reasons.

## What to offer next

If the prospect wants implementation, first map their current lead sources and tools. Do not sell a complicated automation stack before confirming where inquiries actually arrive and who owns follow-up.

# C. Gym Membership Growth Sample

Use for replies to sequence 904610.

## Short-form video concept: "The workout you don't need to be in shape to start"

**Goal:** remove the intimidation objection and create a local membership inquiry.

**Length:** 20–25 seconds.

**0–2 sec hook:**
"Think you need to get in shape *before* joining a gym? That's backwards."

**2–8 sec:**
Show 3 quick, approachable movements or stations while on-screen text says: "Start where you are."

**8–15 sec:**
Trainer/member voiceover: "A good first week isn't about destroying yourself. It's learning the room, finding a repeatable plan and leaving knowing you can come back."

**15–21 sec:**
Show one clear beginner-friendly next step that the gym truly offers: tour, intro session, trial, class consult or coach conversation. Do not invent a free trial if none exists.

**21–25 sec CTA:**
"Message {{company}} with **START** and we'll tell you the easiest first step."

## Inquiry follow-up sequence

### Immediate

Hey {{first_name_or_there}} — thanks for reaching out to {{company}}. What are you mainly trying to improve right now: strength, fat loss, conditioning, confidence in the gym, or something else?

### Day 2

Just checking back — if you're still thinking about getting started, I can point you toward the best first step based on your goal and schedule. What days/times usually work for you?

### Day 5

A lot of people wait because they think they need to be "ready" first. You don't. If you want, reply **START** and we'll make the first visit simple.

### Day 10

Last check-in so we don't keep filling your inbox. If you still want info from {{company}}, reply **OPEN** and we'll keep the conversation going. Otherwise we'll close it out.

## Content variations to build after the first test

- "What your first 30 minutes here actually looks like"
- "3 things beginners worry about that nobody in the gym cares about"
- "Pick your goal: stronger / leaner / less winded / more consistent"
- Coach reaction to a common local fitness myth
- Member journey framed around consistency, not an unverified dramatic transformation
