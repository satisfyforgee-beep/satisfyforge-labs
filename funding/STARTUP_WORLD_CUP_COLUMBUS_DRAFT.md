## One-line pitch
SatisfyForge helps local service businesses turn content and incoming leads into booked jobs by combining short-form marketing, follow-up automation and lightweight business tools in one practical growth system.

## Problem
Small service businesses often buy disconnected marketing tools, post inconsistently, and lose leads after the first call or quote. They do not need more dashboards; they need a simple system that creates demand, follows up, and helps them understand whether the work is profitable.

## Solution
SatisfyForge combines done-for-you short-form content, lead follow-up workflows, calculators and microtools. The service model creates near-term cash flow and customer learning. The successful repeatable workflows are then productized into a simple subscription platform for local service businesses.

## Early validation / traction
No paying customer revenue is claimed yet. The first regional customer-discovery search identified 844 home-service companies in the Cincinnati-Dayton-Middletown area matching the initial target profile. The go-to-market system, free-sample offer, outreach templates and initial digital products are already built. The next milestone is first paid-customer validation and measurement of which niche/offer converts best.

## Beachhead customer
Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, cleaning/restoration, landscaping, pressure washing, remodeling, flooring, detailing and other local service companies where one incremental booked job can justify the cost of SatisfyForge.

## Revenue model
- $299 starter content + lead-follow-up package.
- $599-$999 recurring monthly service target.
- $7-$15 digital products.
- Custom microtools and business calculators.
- Future SaaS subscription for saved projects, niche modules, onboarding and automation.
- Affiliate/content revenue as owned distribution grows.

## Go-to-market
1. Personalized outbound to high-value local service niches.
2. Free sample or diagnostic to reduce buyer risk.
3. Convert results into case studies and niche-specific templates.
4. Use free tools and content as inbound lead magnets.
5. Productize the best workflows into SaaS modules.
6. Expand beyond Ohio once customer acquisition and retention are repeatable.

## Competitive advantage
SatisfyForge is designed around booked-job economics rather than social-media vanity metrics. It combines content, follow-up and lightweight software instead of forcing a small-business owner to buy and manage several systems. The founder also has hands-on restoration/cleaning industry experience, which helps keep the product grounded in practical service-business workflows.
