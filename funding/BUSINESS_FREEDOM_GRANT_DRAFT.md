## Business idea / what are you building?
SatisfyForge is an AI-powered growth business built to help local service companies turn more of their existing attention and inquiries into booked work. Instead of selling another complicated marketing dashboard, I am combining practical short-form content, lead follow-up systems, simple calculators and lightweight business tools. I am starting with contractors and service companies such as roofing, HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, restoration, landscaping and similar businesses, where one additional booked job can justify the cost of the service. The long-term goal is to take the repeatable workflows that work for clients and turn them into a simple subscription software platform that small businesses can actually use.

## What progress have you made?
I have already built a working SatisfyForge Growth Toolbox prototype in GitHub, along with four specialized microtools: a water-restoration estimate planner, an overtime/double-time paycheck calculator, a 3D-print profit calculator and a short-form hook generator. I have also completed two digital products, the Contractor Lead Follow-Up Kit and the Short-Form Hook Vault. For the initial Ohio market, I researched and identified 844 home-service companies in the Cincinnati, Dayton and Middletown area that match the target customer profile. I have built the first prospecting system, outreach templates and a low-risk free-sample offer. I have not claimed paying customer revenue yet; the next goal is converting the first customers and learning which offer produces repeatable results.

## How would you use the $1,000 grant?
I would use the grant to move SatisfyForge from a working prototype into a more legitimate, customer-ready business. The priority would be business registration and basic legal/operational setup, a dedicated domain and hosted website/SaaS infrastructure, and customer-acquisition testing. I would also use part of the funding for the software and creative-production costs needed to deliver high-quality samples and measure which offers convert. The goal would not be to spend the money on unnecessary subscriptions. Every dollar would be tied to either making the business legitimate, making the product easier to use, or getting enough real customer feedback to prove a repeatable sales model.

## Why is the plan feasible?
The business can start with a service model that requires very little upfront capital. The current starter offer is $299 for a small package of vertical videos, social posts and a 30-day content/follow-up plan, with a recurring service target of $599-$999 per month. Digital products can be sold at low prices while the audience grows, and custom tools can be built for individual businesses. That lets SatisfyForge earn cash from services first, then reinvest into the software product instead of needing a large amount of outside capital before the business can make money.

## What impact would the grant have?
The grant would shorten the distance between the current prototype and the first repeatable paying customers. It would allow me to set up the business correctly, give prospects a more professional place to evaluate the offer, and test customer acquisition without putting household money at risk. If the model proves itself, the same system can eventually help small service businesses that cannot afford a traditional agency get better content, stronger follow-up and simpler growth tools at a more accessible price.

## Why does this matter to you personally?
I have spent years around hands-on work and service businesses, including restoration and cleaning work, and I understand the difference between ideas that sound good online and work that actually has to produce a result for a customer. I want to build something I own that can create long-term income instead of relying only on hourly work. SatisfyForge is my attempt to combine practical industry experience with AI and software in a way that helps other small businesses grow while I build a real asset of my own.
