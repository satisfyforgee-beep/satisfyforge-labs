# Cleaning & Restoration Funding — Current Research

Updated: 2026-10-13

## Current objective
Prepare Maloney/SatisfyForge restoration-business funding options without taking on debt or creating financial obligations before owner review.

## Best current Ohio support lane: ECDI
ECDI currently lists offices in Cincinnati and Dayton and offers small-business lending, mentoring/training and support. Their loan information states that an entrepreneur can begin by discussing the business/goals and that there is no credit check until a formal application after a conversation with a relationship manager.

Source pages checked 2026-10-13:
- https://www.ecdi.org/locations
- https://www.ecdi.org/loans

### Why this fits the future restoration company
The planned business is equipment-heavy and service-based: carpet cleaning, water/fire/mold restoration, HVAC cleaning, pressure washing, tile/grout, concrete/garage floors, exterior cleaning and related services. A lender/advisor familiar with small-business equipment and working-capital needs is more useful than chasing generic internet grants.

### Do next without financial commitment
- Keep a complete equipment/startup budget ready.
- Keep a minimum-viable launch plan separate from the full truckmount/restoration build.
- Maintain service pricing, realistic job volume assumptions and monthly fixed-cost model.
- Prepare proof of relevant restoration/carpet/mold experience.
- Prepare a concise use-of-funds schedule showing exactly what each funding dollar buys.
- Use free advising/mentoring before any formal credit application when available.

### Owner-review gate
Do NOT submit a formal loan application, authorize a credit pull, accept a loan, sign guarantees, or commit to equipment purchases without explicit owner review.

## ECDI Small Business Catalyst Fund
ECDI currently advertises a Small Business Catalyst Fund offering working-capital financing from $5,000 to $350,000 at a stated 7.5% fixed interest rate and 60-month term for eligible businesses in select areas. This is debt, not a grant, so it is only a research option—not an automatic recommendation.

Source checked 2026-10-13:
- https://www.ecdi.org/catalyst-fund

## Current funding strategy
1. Free SBDC/ECDI advising and plan review.
2. Validate demand and pricing with low-cost service paths first where practical.
3. Improve business plan/use-of-funds package.
4. Compare responsible microloan/equipment financing only after cash-flow assumptions are defensible.
5. Avoid expensive debt simply to make the business look bigger earlier.

## Zero-spend rule
No application fees, paid grant databases, paid courses, business-credit packages, or financing commitments are authorized by this file.
